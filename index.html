Who I Am

I am a self-motivated, competent, and confident professional with 16 years of teaching and hands-on experience in Computer Science, Windows Server Administration, Networks, Security, IT Operations, and Project Management.

Currently serving as Lecturer / Associate Professor at South East Technological University (SETU), Waterford, Ireland, I bring together deep technical expertise with a genuine passion for education and research. I hold a PhD in Wireless Sensor Networks & Machine Learning, am a Chartered Engineer (CEng) with the Engineering Council UK, and a Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT).

I can manage and handle multi-disciplinary projects, budgets, and teams to achieve assigned goals, and I have the ability to build strong relationships with people and meet tight timelines across 6 countries – Malaysia, Oman, Norway, China, Australia, and Ireland.
